# Day 1: Getting Started

Course: RoboLink: Build and Drive Your Smartphone-Controlled Robot  
Program: Mini Course Program 2026, Carleton University  
Instructors: Dr. Mahmoud Sayed and Mahmoud Yassin

## Overview

Day 1 introduces the RoboLink mini course and the basic electronics ideas students need before programming the Arduino. Students meet the robot project, review important parts, and practise reading simple circuit diagrams.

This day is designed for students who are new to electronics and robotics.

## Learning Goals

By the end of Day 1, students should be able to:

- Describe the goal of the RoboLink robot project.
- Identify common electronics parts used in the course.
- Explain the difference between power, ground, input, and output.
- Recognize the Arduino Nano and the main pins used in later labs.
- Follow basic circuit safety rules before connecting power.
- Prepare for Day 2, where students start using the Arduino IDE.

## Suggested Order

1. Open `Day1_RoboLink_Basic_Electronics.pdf`.
2. Read the course introduction and robot project overview.
3. Review the basic electronics parts and vocabulary.
4. Complete the student activities and reflection questions.
5. Use `Labs/Analog Discovery 2.pdf` if your instructor includes the extra activity.
6. Keep the Day 1 notes ready for Day 2.

## Safety Reminders

- Ask your instructor to check wiring before connecting power.
- Do not connect 5V directly to GND.
- Use resistors with LEDs.
- Disconnect power before changing a circuit.
- Keep wires neat so mistakes are easier to find.
